How To Request Account Deletion

You can request permanent deletion of your Task Flow account by email.

  1. Send an email to jahnviaghera@gmail.com from your registered account email.
  2. Use the subject line: Delete My Task Flow Account.
  3. Include your full name and registered phone number (if used in the app).
  4. Our support team will verify your request and confirm deletion.

Deletion requests are processed within 7 business days.

Data Deleted After Confirmation

  • Your profile details and account settings
  • Assigned task participation data tied directly to your user account
  • Uploaded personal files linked only to your account

Data That May Be Retained

  • Audit, legal, or fraud-prevention logs required by law
  • Records needed to resolve disputes or enforce agreements
  • Aggregated, non-identifiable analytics

Retention is limited to legal, security, and compliance purposes only.

Contact

If you need help with deletion, contact: jahnviaghera@gmail.com
